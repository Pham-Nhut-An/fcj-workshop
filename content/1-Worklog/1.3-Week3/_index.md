### Week 3 Objectives:

* Understand managed relational databases (RDS), NoSQL databases (DynamoDB), and in-memory caching.
* Learn the concepts of High Availability and Auto Scaling with Elastic Load Balancing.

### Tasks to be carried out this week:
| Day | Task | Start Date | Completion Date | Reference Material |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

| 3 | - Learn Amazon DynamoDB (NoSQL): <br> &emsp; + Tables, Items, Attributes <br> &emsp; + Primary Keys: Partition Key & Composite Key (Partition + Sort) <br> &emsp; + Global Secondary Indexes (GSI) and Local Secondary Indexes (LSI) <br> &emsp; + Provisioned vs On-Demand capacity modes. | 29/09/2026 | 29/09/2026 | <https://cloudjourney.awsstudygroup.com/> |

### Week 3 Achievements:

* Learned the basics of Amazon RDS (Multi-AZ, Read Replicas) and DynamoDB data modeling.
* Learned the concept of in-memory caching with ElastiCache.
* Learned the concept of a 3-tier web application using ALB, Auto Scaling Group, and RDS (hands-on practice pending).
