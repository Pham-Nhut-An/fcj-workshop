# Sharing and Feedback

### Overall Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**
The AWS Vietnam office is comfortable and quiet, good for focusing on study and the workshop. Working on a real AWS account helped me understand how the services actually run and how costs are incurred.

**2. Support from Mentor / Team Admin**
The mentor and the FCAJ team answered our questions when the team got stuck and clearly published the scoring and submission rules. Letting students find a solution first helped me learn more than just following an answer.

**3. Relevance of Work to Academic Major**
I study Artificial Intelligence, so the AI services (Amazon Bedrock, Amazon Rekognition) matched my major closely. I also learned areas I had never worked on: cloud infrastructure, IAM security, serverless and monitoring.

**4. Learning & Skill Development Opportunities**
I learned to read AWS documentation, design an architecture following AWS Well-Architected, estimate costs with the Pricing Calculator, troubleshoot deployments, write technical documentation and work in a team. The final workshop is where I learned the most, because I had to fix real problems myself.

**5. Company Culture & Team Spirit**
Events such as AWS Cloud and AI Day and FCAJ Community Day let me meet people from the industry and other interns. In our team, members agreed on one topic and shared documents with each other.

**6. Internship Policies / Benefits**
Interns can use the office, join AWS and community events, and have learning material available on First Cloud Journey.

---

### Additional Questions

* **Most satisfying:** deploying a real system on AWS myself, from architecture design to security testing and monitoring.
* **What should be improved for future interns:**
  * New accounts have an AI service quota (Amazon Bedrock) of 0 and the quota increase request was denied, so our team had to change topic midway. An early warning or a lab account would save time.
  * Submission rules should be announced earlier so teams can prepare the summary and architecture files from the start.
  * The AWS Console has changed compared with some lab guides; screenshots and instructions should be updated.
* **Would I recommend it to a friend:** Yes. The program offers hands-on practice on real AWS, mentor support and events to learn more. I would advise friends to learn the basics first and keep a steady pace from week one.

---

### Suggestions & Expectations

* **Suggestion:** add a mid-term architecture review so teams can fix issues early, before deploying.
* **Would I continue the program:** not decided yet; it depends on my internship result and my university schedule.
* **Other comments:** thank you to the FCAJ team and mentors for the chance to learn and work on a real project.
