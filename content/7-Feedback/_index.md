### Overall impression of the FCAJ internship program

The program took me from AWS fundamentals to deploying a real system on my own AWS account. The final workshop taught me the most, because I had to solve real errors instead of only following a guide.

### Satisfaction level

Satisfied.

### Points to improve

* New accounts had a quota of 0 for AI services (Amazon Bedrock) and the increase request was denied, so our team had to change topics midway. An early warning or lab accounts would save time.
* The submission rules could be published earlier so teams can prepare the summary and architecture files from the start.
* The AWS Console has changed compared with some lab guides; screenshots and steps should be refreshed.

### Would you recommend the program to friends? Why?

Yes. It offers hands-on practice on real AWS, mentor support and events to learn from. I would advise friends to study the fundamentals beforehand and keep a steady pace from the first week.
