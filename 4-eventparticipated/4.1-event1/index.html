Event 1

Summary Report: “AWS Cloud and AI Day Hanoi 2026”

Event Information

  • Event name: AWS Cloud and AI Day Hanoi 2026
  • Date & time: Tuesday, 29/09/2026, 07:30 – 17:00 (UTC+7)
  • Main venue: JW Marriott Hotel Hanoi
  • Where I attended: HCMC Watch Party – AWS Vietnam office, Ho Chi Minh City (live stream of the keynote and selected sessions)
  • Role: Attendee
  • Event page: https://aws.amazon.com/vi/events/cloud-day-hanoi/

Event Objectives

  • Show how AWS helps builders turn ideas into real products with cloud and AI
  • Introduce agentic AI (AI agents that can plan and carry out tasks) for developers, workload modernization and security
  • Share real customer stories from Vietnamese companies
  • Connect developers, IT leaders and the AWS community in Vietnam

Keynote Speakers

  • H.E. Jennifer Wicks – U.S. Ambassador to Vietnam
  • Dr. Werner Vogels – CTO, Amazon.com
  • Eric Yeo – Country General Manager Malaysia, Vietnam, Indochina, AWS
  • Mai Le – Director of Product Management, Katalon
  • Dr. Jens Lottner – Chief Executive Officer, Techcombank

Sessions I Attended

Key Highlights

Keynote: making “impossible” ideas possible

  • AWS presented how its tools and resources help builders go beyond their limits, with experiences from customers such as Techcombank and Katalon.
  • A big theme was agentic systems: AI agents that support developers, help modernize workloads and help keep organizations secure.

Agentic AI for software development

  • AI is moving from “suggesting code” to agents that can take a task, plan the steps and carry them out (write code, write tests, fix errors).
  • The developer’s role shifts toward describing the problem clearly, reviewing the agent’s output and making the final decisions.

AI-ready data foundation

  • An AI application is only as good as its data: data needs to be collected, cleaned, organized and governed before it can be used by AI.
  • The cloud provides managed services for storing and analyzing data so teams can focus on using it instead of maintaining infrastructure.

Migration at scale with AWS Transform

  • AWS Transform uses agentic AI to speed up migrating and modernizing large, old systems, a job that normally takes a lot of manual work.

Key Takeaways

  • AI is now a practical tool in real Vietnamese businesses, not just a research topic.
  • Good AI starts with good data and a solid cloud foundation (security, cost control, monitoring).
  • As an AI student, I need to learn both AI and cloud: models are only useful when they are deployed, monitored and secured in a real system.

Applying to My Work

  • Use the idea of an AI-ready data flow (upload → process → store → use) when designing our team’s capstone workshop.
  • Prefer managed / serverless AWS services so the team can focus on the AI features instead of managing servers.
  • Try AI coding assistants carefully: use them to speed up work, but always read and understand the generated code.

Event Experience

This was my first large AWS event. Watching the keynote and sessions together with other FCAJ interns at the HCMC Watch Party helped me see how cloud and AI are used by real companies in Vietnam. Many terms were new to me (agentic AI, data foundation, AWS Transform), so I noted them down to study further during the internship.
